What We Do

Services for Every Property Need

From a single repair to a full renovation, we've got you covered. Here's what we can take off your plate.

🏗️

General Contracting

Taking on a bigger project? Renovations, room additions, remodels — we manage the whole thing start to finish, so you don't have to stress the details. Great for investors and homeowners planning upgrades.

🔨

Repairs & Maintenance

Got a list of things that need fixing? We'll work through it efficiently and leave the property in great shape — no job is too small to deserve quality attention.

📷

Smart Home & Security Cameras

We install security cameras, smart doorbells, and home automation systems — professionally mounted and configured. A popular upgrade for rental properties and homes going on the market.

📺

TV Mounting

Clean walls, no guesswork. We'll mount your TV securely on any surface — drywall, brick, or concrete — and tuck the cables away so everything looks sharp and finished.

💡

Electrical & Lighting

New outlets, ceiling fans, recessed lighting, fixture swaps — we handle it safely and to code. A lighting refresh can do wonders for a home you're staging or a rental you're turning over.

🔧

Plumbing Fixture Upgrades

Faucets, toilets, showerheads, sinks — we replace and upgrade plumbing fixtures quickly and cleanly. Simple updates that go a long way in kitchens and bathrooms.

🎨

Drywall Repair & Painting

Holes, cracks, scuffs, water damage — we patch it all and paint it perfectly. One of the fastest ways to refresh a property before a sale, listing, or new tenant move-in.
